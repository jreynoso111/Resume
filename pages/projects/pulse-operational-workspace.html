Pulse Operational Workspace

Pulse is an operational work management platform that combines portfolio intelligence, configurable boards, geospatial service discovery, and in-product learning in one focused workspace.

Platform Overview

Pulse was designed as a responsive command layer for teams coordinating many workstreams at once. Workspace summaries surface volume, completion, deadlines, and risk, while each board provides the view best suited to the work. The result is a clearer operating rhythm without forcing teams into a single workflow.

Core Capabilities

  • Operational overview: Portfolio-level signals for open work, completion, deadlines, workload, and items requiring attention.
  • Flexible execution views: Table, Kanban, Gantt, and calendar modes for different planning and delivery needs.
  • Connected work: Import and export tools, relationships, sharing controls, and configurable board structures.
  • Service and learning layers: A map-based HUB for service discovery and a role-aware guide that teaches users inside the live workspace.

Key Product Surfaces

Configurable Boards

Move between operational views, manage relationships, and exchange data without leaving the board context.

Operational Intelligence

Turn board activity into a concise view of throughput, risk, workload, and near-term commitments.

Service Discovery HUB

Search a nationwide service network by location and category from an interactive geospatial workspace.

Operational Impact

Pulse consolidates portfolio monitoring, board execution, service discovery, and user enablement into one system. This reduces tool switching, standardizes follow-up, and gives teams a shared view of priorities and delivery risk while preserving flexibility at the board level.
